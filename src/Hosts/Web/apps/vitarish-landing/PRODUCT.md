## Product Purpose

Vitarish is a technology brand built around a simple purpose: help people make room for the work that matters. Its public landing page introduces the brand, explains its current product, and gives visitors a direct route to that product.

This is the Vitarish brand site. Darman is its current public product, not a replacement name for Vitarish.

## Users

Visitors discovering Vitarish and people looking for its products. The current product introduction serves private-practice owners and teams who want to understand Darman and reach its website.

Do not describe audiences for products that have not been approved for public communication.

## Public Product Scope

Owner decision, 2026-10-27 (revised): **Darman is the only available product.** Mention it in one body section and as the sole active product link in the footer. Keep the hero, navigation, metadata, and brand FAQ free of repeated product promotion.

- Introduce Darman as Vitarish's current product for private-practice management.
- Link directly to [Darman](https://darman.vitarish.com).
- Describe existing practice workflows concisely: appointments, shifts and services, patient records and care timelines, and staff roles.
- Preserve the configured practice-setup action when available.
- Keep detailed product explanation on Darman's own site.

Explicitly approved footer-only placeholders: gym management and beauty salon management under For Businesses; Persian Etymology Agent and File converter under For Individuals. Display these as plain text with purple Soon chips, with no links or actions. Darman has a green Ready chip and links to its site. This exception does not authorize other unannounced directions or imply that the placeholders work. The landing feature maps Soon to the host-owned tertiary chroma token using the shared soft-chip recipe.

Before adding a product, obtain an explicit owner decision that it is approved for public mention, record its release or announcement status here, and verify its public destination and claims. Implementation in the repository alone is not publication approval.

## Positioning and Voice

Lead with Vitarish and its slogan, “The glory of purpose.” Express the brand's belief in purposeful technology through clear language and a distinctive visual identity.

Use Darman as the concrete expression of that purpose today. Avoid broad capability claims, promises of future expansion, technical jargon, and generic AI positioning without an approved, specific product benefit.

## Visitor Narrative

1. Vitarish identity, animated artwork, slogan, and a short introduction.
2. A concise statement of the brand's purpose.
3. Darman: what it is, who it serves, and where to discover it.
4. Essential brand questions and configured human contact.
5. A compact, muted footer inspired by the supplied Apple reference, with product columns and a copyright row; no oversized wordmark or repeated product CTA.

The primary visitor decision is whether to explore Darman. The page is not a product roadmap or an operational dashboard.

## Visual Direction

Owner decision, 2026-10-27: expressive animated artwork and a strong Vitarish identity.

Use the existing logotypes, theme, typography, and fiber artwork. Give the wordmark a prominent role, keep generous space around the brand statement, and use a focused product presentation rather than a catalog grid. Art must support readable content in both themes.

Reuse shared marketing actions, HeroUI interactions, and the shared viewport-reveal behavior. Keep a static, readable page when animation or WebGL is unavailable. Provide a localized animation pause control and honor reduced-motion preferences.

## Approved Commercial and Contact Facts

- Pricing (owner decision, 2026-10-22): a clinic can run for free with no time limit. Do not extend this to every feature or invent prices and plan limits.
- Human contact (owner decision, 2026-10-22): WhatsApp, usually replying within an hour during working hours. Show contact only when configured.

Support English and Persian throughout, including metadata, tooltips, navigation, and FAQs. Preserve RTL layout and the existing language-specific fonts. Ensure keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, mobile reflow, semantic headings, and reduced-motion behavior.
