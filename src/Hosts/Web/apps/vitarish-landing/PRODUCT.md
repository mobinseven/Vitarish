## Platform

## Users

Vitarish is intended for everyone using internet services, with small businesses as the primary commercial audience. Current users include private-practice offices using Vitarish's business-management applications. Future business audiences include spa and beauty salons and e-shops. The landing page should also make Vitarish understandable to people discovering its common-purpose tools, such as an AI-assisted dictionary.

## Product Purpose

Vitarish is a multipurpose, AI-native technology platform built to help people and organizations achieve their purpose. Its current product focus is practical management software for small businesses, beginning with private-practice offices. The landing page should communicate the platform and spotlight a small number of features or tools that can be updated daily.

## Positioning

Vitarish is purpose-oriented rather than competitor-oriented: its services are meant to help anyone achieve their purpose, including ordinary internet users and organizations that might otherwise be considered rivals. Its platform combines focused small-business applications with broadly useful AI-native tools.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive to understand Vitarish as a company, discover a current spotlight feature, or find a useful service. The landing page needs to support a minimal presentation with regularly refreshed spotlight content while remaining extensible as new applications and tools launch.
