# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the person in charge of a private-practice office. They oversee patient routines and ensure that care is tracked and documented properly. They also coordinate office doctors and other experts, managing appointments and agendas in real time.

## Product Purpose

Vitarish helps private-practice operators run patient and office workflows in one place. It supports tracking and documenting patient routines, coordinating providers and appointments, defining shifts and services, and creating prescriptions manually or from predefined templates for submission to insurance providers. Success means the operator can keep patient progress and office operations current without important details being lost in the daily volume of incoming data.

## Positioning

Vitarish is modern and AI-native. Its distinct mechanism is guiding the operator toward a meaningful grasp of each patient's progress in their routines, rather than acting only as a data-acquisition or record-storage system.

## Operating Context

The product is used during the daily operation of a private practice: monitoring patient routines, documenting progress, managing doctors and other experts, coordinating appointments and agendas in real time, defining shifts and services, managing providers, and preparing prescriptions for insurance submission.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Patient routines must be tracked and documented.
- Patient progress must remain understandable despite high daily data volume.
- Providers, doctors, experts, services, shifts, appointments, and agendas must be manageable in real time.
- Prescriptions can be entered manually or selected from predefined templates, then sent to insurance providers.
- The binding product principle is KISS (Keep It Simple Stupid): avoid multilevel menus, multilevel directions, and overcomplicated forms. Workflows should be stupidly simple.
- The existing application is a localized web app with English and Persian message sets; right-to-left behavior is an existing technical consideration.

## Brand Commitments

The product is called Vitarish. It should communicate a modern, AI-native product without sacrificing clarity or simplicity.

## Product Principles

- Make the next meaningful action obvious.
- Preserve the clinical story, not just the incoming data.
- Keep routine office work fast and comprehensible in real time.
- Prefer simple, shallow workflows over feature-dense navigation.
- Automate and guide where it reduces cognitive load, while keeping operators in control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The product must remain usable in both English and Persian, including right-to-left presentation where applicable. Simplicity is an inclusion requirement: avoid unnecessary steps, dense navigation hierarchies, and complex forms.
