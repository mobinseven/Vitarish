# No-Show Handling (MVP)

## Goal
Close unattended appointments consistently.

## Trigger
Patient does not arrive within grace period.

## Steps (MVP)
1. Mark appointment as no-show.
2. Record no-show reason/category.
3. Trigger policy action (fee, warning, or rebooking prompt).
4. Optionally create follow-up task.

## Output
No-show is finalized with policy and audit trail applied.

## Out of scope
General daily schedule review and manual booking creation.
