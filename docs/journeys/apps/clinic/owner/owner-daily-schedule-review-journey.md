# Daily Schedule Review (MVP)

## Goal
Review and prepare today’s clinic schedule.

## Trigger
Start of business day.

## Steps (MVP)
1. Open today view.
2. Review totals by status (confirmed, waiting, cancelled).
3. Identify gaps/overbook risks.
4. Assign follow-up actions to staff.

## Output
Daily plan is confirmed with clear action list.

## Out of scope
Individual appointment edits and no-show closure actions.
